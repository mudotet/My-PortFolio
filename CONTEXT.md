# Portfolio

This portfolio presents Tú Phan's software-engineering work and experience to prospective employers.

## Language

**Primary audience**:
Software-engineering recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Tú Phan as a candidate.
_Avoid_: General visitors, customers

**Experience bar**:
A fast-loading portfolio with fluid scrolling and interactions on a mid-range phone and a typical laptop, while honoring reduced-motion preferences.
_Avoid_: Animation added solely to improve an audit score

**Showcase moment**:
A brief, product-launch-style welcome that leads into an engineering-forward hero and interactive project case studies.
_Avoid_: Decorative motion that obscures the portfolio work

**Welcome sequence**:
A 1–2 second, skippable entry sequence shown once per browser session; it is omitted when reduced motion is preferred.
_Avoid_: A recurring or blocking intro

**Welcome copy**:
The welcome sequence presents `Tú Phan`, then `Software Engineer`, then `Welcome to my work.` as separate steps.

**Welcome palette**:
The welcome gradient uses the portfolio's navy-and-orange palette.
_Avoid_: A separate Apple-style aurora palette

**Motion hierarchy**:
The welcome sequence is the cinematic moment; subsequent motion is interaction-led, using reveals and hover or tap feedback rather than continuous decoration.
_Avoid_: Continuous decorative animation after entry

**Project card behavior**:
Project cards keep their problem, approach, and impact content visible for fast recruiter scanning; interaction emphasizes the existing system-flow row.
_Avoid_: Click-through detail views or hidden case-study content

**Upgrade scope**:
The performance and motion upgrade preserves the portfolio's current sections and copy, focusing on their delivery rather than a content rewrite.
