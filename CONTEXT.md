# Portfolio

This portfolio presents Tú Phan's software-engineering work and experience to prospective employers.

## Language

**Primary audience**:
Software-engineering recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Tú Phan as a candidate.
_Avoid_: General visitors, customers

**Experience bar**:
A fast-loading portfolio with fluid scrolling and interactions on a mid-range phone and a typical laptop, while honoring reduced-motion preferences.
_Avoid_: Animation added solely to improve an audit score

**Showcase moment**:
A brief, product-launch-style welcome that leads into an engineering-forward hero and interactive project case studies.
_Avoid_: Decorative motion that obscures the portfolio work

**Welcome sequence**:
A 1–2 second neo-brutalist poster slam shown once per browser session; it is skippable and omitted when reduced motion is preferred.
_Avoid_: A recurring or blocking intro

**Welcome copy**:
The welcome sequence presents `Phan Thanh Tu`, then `Software Engineer`, then `Welcome to my work.` as separate steps.

**Welcome palette**:
The welcome sequence uses hard cuts from the portfolio's cream, red, yellow, violet, black, and white palette.
_Avoid_: Gradients, soft cinematic effects, a separate intro palette

**Motion hierarchy**:
The welcome sequence is the cinematic moment; subsequent motion is interaction-led except for one slow skills conveyor that pauses on hover or focus and becomes static when reduced motion is preferred.
_Avoid_: Other continuous animation after entry

**Project card behavior**:
Project cards keep their problem, approach, and impact content visible for fast recruiter scanning; interaction emphasizes the existing system-flow row.
_Avoid_: Click-through detail views or hidden case-study content

**Upgrade scope**:
The performance and motion upgrade preserves the portfolio's current sections and copy, focusing on their delivery rather than a content rewrite.

**Visual-system replacement**:
A portfolio-wide restyle that preserves the existing sections, copy, resume and certificate previews, contact form, and project behavior while replacing the visual language consistently.
_Avoid_: Partial reskin, feature redesign

**Palette mode**:
The portfolio uses one light neo-brutalist palette; theme switching is not part of the portfolio experience.
_Avoid_: Dark mode, theme toggle

**Engineering dossier**:
The portfolio's personal art direction: neo-brutalist print and sticker treatments organized around backend-system diagrams, graph-paper structure, status stamps, and an identity-card portrait.
_Avoid_: Generic neo-brutalist template, decorative sticker collage without engineering meaning

**Chaos zones**:
High-density composition is concentrated in the welcome, hero, section headings, and project diagrams; qualifications and contact content remain on a strict, readable grid.
_Avoid_: Uniform visual noise, restrained styling everywhere

**Evidence order**:
The portfolio presents Hero, About and Credentials, Projects, Experience, Skills, then Contact so verified credibility precedes applied work and professional depth.
_Avoid_: Projects before credentials, biography-first sequencing

**Flagship case file**:
GuideLens AI is the full-width project centerpiece; the other three projects are equal supporting dossiers.
_Avoid_: Four equally weighted projects, HUMG Regulation AI or Identity Services as the visual flagship

**Portrait treatment**:
The existing portrait remains recognizable and full-color inside a rotated identity-card frame; halftone texture belongs to the surrounding panel rather than the face.
_Avoid_: Black-and-white portrait, heavily posterized face, replacement studio image

**Typography roles**:
Space Grotesk carries headings, body text, and actions; the native monospace stack is reserved for project identifiers, dates, system-flow labels, and status stamps.
_Avoid_: A second web font, monospace body copy
